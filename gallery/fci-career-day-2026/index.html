MUCOSA Career Day 2026
Career inspiration & community

2026 Mbarara, Uganda

The MBARARA UNIVERSITY COMPUTING STUDENTS ASSOCIATION, MUCOSA Career Day 2026 at Mbarara University of Science & Technology was absolutely massive.

Industry pioneers, same classrooms

What made this event truly special was realizing that the industry pioneers speaking to us stood exactly where we are standing today. Ten to twenty years ago, they were students sitting in these very same spaces — spaces that have now evolved into developed faculties. An inspiring reminder of what is possible.

War-room: handling a ransomware breach

Dr. Fred Kaggwa (Dean of Faculty) turned a career talk into a practical war-room scenario. We tackled a mock ransomware attack on a hospital network and mapped out the three definitive steps to handle a breach:

Isolate. Contain the blast radius before it spreads.

Escalate. Bring in the right people, fast.

Analyze. Understand the root cause so it never happens again.

Master the concepts, not just the cram

Lasto Mubiru (Senior System Admin) reminded us that networking and cybersecurity go hand-in-hand. His golden rule for long-term tech success? Master the concepts, don't just cram them. He also gave us a lighthearted reality check from his own experience — confessing there were times he would end up falling asleep completely by mistake because of the grind. A funny but real moment that showed just how dedicated you have to be.

A day of connections

It was a valuable opportunity to connect with fellow students, engage with the tech community, and gain insights into career opportunities, professional development, and the ever-evolving world of technology. Events like these remind us that building a successful career goes beyond academic knowledge. It also takes continuous learning, networking, collaboration, and the willingness to explore new opportunities.

Key takeaway of the day: Your career starts before you get a job.

Photo moment

To wrap up an incredible day, I captured a photo moment with MUCOSA President Mubiru Elton Felix and a friend to anchor the memories of a day well spent — grateful for their acceptance to share the moment.

The building blocks are right here. If you want to build yourself, the time to start is now. Here's to learning, growing, and building towards a brighter future in technology!
