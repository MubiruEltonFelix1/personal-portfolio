Edge AI on Android:
Gemini Nano, Gemma & LiteRT

October 23–24, 2026 Mbarara, Uganda

I’m preparing for my first conference talk at DevFest Mbarara 2026: a practical look at running generative AI models directly on Android instead of sending every request to a remote server.

The session, “Edge AI on Android: Running Local Gemini Nano & Gemma Models with LiteRT,” explores what changes when inference happens on the device. Local processing can keep prompts and data on the phone, reduce dependence on a network round trip, and make AI features feel more immediate.

What the talk is about

We’ll look at the building blocks behind on-device AI on Android: compact Gemini Nano and Gemma models, the LiteRT runtime, and the practical considerations that come with running machine-learning workloads within a mobile app.

AI on the phone

What it means to run model inference locally on an Android device.

Privacy by design

How local processing can keep user data on the device.

LiteRT in the stack

Where LiteRT fits when bringing Gemini Nano or Gemma into Android experiences.

The central idea: useful AI does not always need to live in the cloud. On-device inference opens a path to more private, responsive mobile experiences.
