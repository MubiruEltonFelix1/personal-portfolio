Build With AI Makerere Hackathon

A weekend of building, learning, and connecting — the Build With AI Makerere Hackathon brought together developers, designers, and dreamers at the American Center Kampala. Organised by Shakiran Nannyombi and Benjamin Wakida Eldon, in partnership with Major League Hacking and GDG on Campus — Makerere University.

Result

Our project — MoMo Scam Checker — secured 2nd place at the hackathon!

The Team — Tech Clan

  • Joy Wabule
  • Awany Jey Daniel
  • LUBEGA Grace
  • Mubiru Elton Felix

Our team, Tech Clan, came together around a shared mission: build something that actually helps people. The result was MoMo Scam Checker, a solution designed to help reduce money lost to scammers across various telecommunication networks in Uganda.

The Experience

Beyond the podium finish, the experience was truly memorable — from the amazing venue and awesome swag to the inspiring people in the room (and, of course, the snacks).

It was also great meeting in person several people I've connected with on LinkedIn but hadn't had the chance to meet face-to-face, including Godwin Ofwono, lailah grant, Ronnie Linslay Atuhaire, Mariah Miranda .L and Byaruhanga Daniel.
