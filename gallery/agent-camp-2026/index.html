Agent Camp 2026

Global AI Mbarara Session 2 kicked off with a deep dive into AI Workflows, Agents, and their practical applications. Spearheaded by lead organiser Joronimo Amanya, the session brought together AI enthusiasts from across Mbarara for an afternoon of learning, live demos, and hands-on coding.

Global AI Mbarara

A local chapter of the Global AI Community, organising meetups, workshops, and hands-on sessions to connect AI practitioners across Mbarara and beyond.

The Speakers

Muhwezi Amon

Founder, Icraddle Innovations

Broke down the core concepts: Large Language Models, Workflows, and AI Agents, explaining the differences between the three. Shared a live repo that the audience cloned, installed dependencies on, and ran together to see results in real time.

Adam Katongole

Team Lead, Psittacus Technologies

Demonstrated how knowledge of workflows was integrated into Virtura, his latest innovation. Showed a production-grade example of AI workflows solving real problems.

Hands-On

The audience followed along with Amon's repo, installing dependencies and running the code during the session. The repo is available at: lnkd.in/de8sjXmD

What We Covered

The session started with foundational theory: what makes a Large Language Model different from a traditional ML model, how Workflows chain multiple AI steps together, and where AI Agents fit as autonomous decision-makers in those workflows. From there, both speakers showed how these concepts move from whiteboard to production.

Adam's Virtura demo was a highlight -- seeing a complete workflow-driven product built on the very principles being taught made the theory click for everyone in the room. The audience asked sharp questions about orchestration, error handling, and when to choose an agent over a fixed workflow.
