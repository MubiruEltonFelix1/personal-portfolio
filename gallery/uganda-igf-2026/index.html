The Project: Diri (Digital Rights)

What Inspired It

Digital governance is one of the most important conversations of our time — yet most young Ugandans never get to be part of it. The language is often technical, the content is buried in policy documents, and the platforms used to share it simply don't reach the people who need it most.

Diri was born from a simple question: how do we make digital governance engaging, accessible, and genuinely useful for every young person in Uganda — regardless of their connectivity, their device, or their ability?

How We Built It

Accessibility-First Platform

Diri delivers engaging content on digital governance with built-in accessibility tools — a screen reader, adjustable font sizes, and a high-contrast setting for youth with special needs.

WhatsApp Multilingual Chatbot

Youth don't need to remember links to get information. A multilingual WhatsApp chatbot brings digital governance conversations to wherever young people already are — no app install required.

USSD Simulator

An embedded USSD simulator makes information accessible to youth without smartphones or internet access, ensuring no one is left out of the digital governance conversation.

Youth Participation

Every feature points back to one goal: increasing the number of youth across the country who can participate in digital governance discussions and easily access this information.

From Hackathon to the National Stage

Following our win, the President of the Internet Society Uganda Chapter, Mr Joshua Mpairwe, invited us to showcase Diri at the Uganda Internet Governance Forum at the Sheraton Kampala Hotel the following day.

In 24 hours, Diri went from a hackathon prototype to a solution presented in front of policymakers, industry leaders, and advocates shaping Uganda's digital future. That invitation was validation that what we built in the hackathon wasn't just a winning demo — it was a genuinely useful tool with a real role to play in Uganda's digital governance landscape.

We didn't just win a hackathon. We built a solution that earned its way to the national conversation on internet governance.

The Conversations That Mattered

Across the Youth IGF hackathon and the main Uganda Internet Governance Forum, discussions and activities focused on the topics that are shaping Uganda's digital future:

Internet Governance

How Uganda's internet is run, who makes the decisions, and how young people can have a seat at that table.

Cybersecurity

Building digital safety awareness and empowering youth to protect themselves and their communities online.

Digital Inclusion

Ensuring every Ugandan — regardless of device, connectivity, or ability — can participate in the digital economy.

Online Gender-Based Violence

Confronting OGBV head-on and working toward a safer online space for women and girls across the country.

Beyond the Code

It was an incredible experience filled with learning, innovation, and meaningful networking with passionate individuals from across the country. From fellow young innovators to policymakers and advocates, every conversation added a new perspective on what Uganda's digital future can look like — and what each of us can do to shape it.
