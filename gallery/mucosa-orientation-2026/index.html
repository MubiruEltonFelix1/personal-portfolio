MUCOSA Orientation 2026

My first ever MUCOSA Friday Session as President of the Mbarara University Computing Students Association (MUCOSA). On Friday, August 21, 2026, the association's first session of the semester doubled as our orientation for the incoming 2026/27 cohort — and it was my honour to lead it.

MUCOSA is the home of computing students at MUST, and its heartbeat is the Friday session: every Friday during the semester, from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM, we meet to learn, share opportunities, and build together. The orientation was the first of those sessions this academic year — the moment we open the doors wide and let the freshers see what they've just joined.

My Role

As president, I presided over the session — opening MUCOSA's year in front of the very people the association exists to serve.

Welcome & Membership Briefing

GDG & the Wider Ecosystem

Leadership stopped being a title the moment I picked up the microphone. It became a room full of freshers counting on me to open doors.

The Friday Ritual

MUCOSA Friday Sessions run every Friday from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM throughout the semester. Some weeks are briefings, some are talks and demos, some are pure community — but every one of them is where computing students at MUST find out what is possible beyond the classroom. If you're a computing student at Mbarara University, that room is yours. Show up.
