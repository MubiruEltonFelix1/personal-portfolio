Notion Community Show Day 2026

My very first Notion Community event, and it did not disappoint. Organised by Ronnie Linslay Atuhaire and Shakiran Nannyombi, with Bahati Brenda Kizito serving as the MC for the day.

Notion Community

A community-driven event series exploring how Notion can transform personal productivity, team collaboration, and creative workflows through hands-on learning and shared experiences.

Highlight

Another Kahoot podium finish hosted by Benjamin Wakida Eldon — bragging rights well earned!

What We Learned

The speakers shared valuable insights covering everything from building stunning dashboards and creating customised Notion avatars to exploring creative ways Notion can boost everyday productivity. Each session was practical, showing real workflows rather than just theory.

The energy in the room was fantastic — people from different universities, different disciplines, all coming together around the idea that better tools enable better thinking.

Connections Made

I was excited to connect with amazing people from across the tech ecosystem, including community leads from various universities such as Victoria University, IUEA, and many others. Events like these remind me how strong Uganda's tech community is growing.

Grateful to everyone who made the space engaging and welcoming.
