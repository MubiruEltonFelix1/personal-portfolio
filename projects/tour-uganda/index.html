// The Problem Planning the Trip Was Harder Than Taking It

Uganda is called the Pearl of Africa for a reason — misty mountain gorillas, wide savannahs, the mighty Nile, and some of the warmest welcomes on the planet. Getting there is the easy part. Planning the trip has always been the hard part.

Would-be travellers end up buried in scattered blogs, dead links, and advice that contradicts itself. What does a day on the road actually cost? Where can you sleep near the parks? Which tour companies can you trust — and how do you even get from the airport to the wildlife? Each answer hides on a different website, or nowhere at all.

So most people either give up halfway through planning, or hand the whole trip — and their whole budget — to somebody else and hope for the best. A dream destination deserved better than browser-tab chaos.
