Overview

Tour Uganda is a comprehensive travel platform that brings together every aspect of planning a trip to Uganda. From an AI-powered trip planner to interactive maps, accommodation directories, and a live cost calculator — everything is designed to give travellers a seamless, informed experience.

Built as a multi-page static application with dark mode, responsive design, and an integrated AI chat assistant for real-time travel Q&A.

Features

AI Trip Planner

Answer 3 simple questions, get a full day-by-day itinerary tailored to your preferences.

Interactive Map

Leaflet-powered map with national parks, gorilla sectors, airports, hospitals, and more.

Destination Guides

8 featured destinations with GPS coordinates, ratings, seasonal calendars, and hidden gems.

Accommodation Directory

Filter lodges and hotels by tier and budget, complete with map view integration.

Tour Operators

Verified operator profiles with specialties, ratings, and direct contact.

Transport Hub

Flights, car rental, private drivers, buses, and boats — all in one place.

Cost Calculator

Live trip estimate broken down by category — accommodation, transport, activities, meals.

Itinerary Builder

Drag-and-drop day planner for building your perfect trip schedule.

Inspiration Gallery

Virtual tours, traveller reviews, and a curated photo gallery.

Dark Mode

Full theme toggle with persisted user preference across all pages.

AI Chat Assistant

In-page travel Q&A chatbot for real-time answers about destinations, transport, and tips.
