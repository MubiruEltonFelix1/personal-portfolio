Overview

TechWrite is a dedicated publishing platform where students and lecturers can write, upload, and share technology articles. Readers can view, comment, save articles for later reading, and receive notifications — creating a vibrant campus tech community.

Built to bridge the gap between classroom learning and the broader tech conversation, TechWrite gives every campus voice a platform to share insights, tutorials, research, and opinions on technology.

Brand Identity

TechWrite is branded around the idea of the write mark — a play on both writing and right — positioning the platform as the definitive place for campus tech thought leadership.

The Write Mark

Every article leaves a mark. TechWrite is where campus tech voices make theirs.

Tech Stack

React, TypeScript, Supabase, Tailwind CSS, Lovable Cloud

For Campus

Built by students, for students and lecturers — a living tech library.

Core Features

Write & Publish

Rich text editor for writing tech articles with support for code blocks, images, and formatting.

Browse & Discover

Category-based browsing, search, and trending articles to help readers discover relevant tech content.

Comments & Discussion

Threaded comments on articles enabling discussion between authors and readers.

Read Later

Save articles to a personal reading list for offline or later consumption.

Notifications

Real-time notifications for new comments, replies, and articles from followed authors.

Role-Based Access

Student and lecturer roles with appropriate permissions for publishing and moderation.
