// The Problem Great campus tech knowledge kept disappearing

Picture a campus full of people figuring technology out at the same time. The best explanations happen in classrooms and group chats — and then they evaporate. Notes scatter across messaging apps, useful write-ups sit in documents nobody can find again, and when a junior student needs that same answer a semester later, it's simply gone.

Meanwhile, lecturers with years of hard-won insight had nowhere easy to share it beyond the lecture hall, and the big global tech blogs rarely speak to a campus reality students actually recognise. Every cohort ended up relearning what the one before it already knew.

What was missing was a single, welcoming place where campus voices could publish technology writing and build on each other's work. That's the gap TechWrite closes — and every article published there leaves what we call the write mark, a nod to both writing well and getting things right.

// The Payoff Everything a campus writer and reader needs

Write without friction

A friendly editor takes care of code blocks, images, and clean formatting, so your energy goes into the ideas — then hit publish when you're proud of it.

Find what matters fast

Browse by category, search for topics, or check what's trending, so relevant tech content finds you instead of hiding from you.

Real discussion, on the page

Threaded comments let readers and authors respond to each other directly under an article — questions asked, answers kept.

Your own reading list

Save any article for later and build a personal library you can return to whenever you're ready, even offline.

Never miss a beat

Get notified in real time when someone comments, replies to you, or publishes something new — including authors you follow.

Everyone in their lane

Student and lecturer accounts each carry the permissions that fit them — covering publishing and moderation — so the platform stays orderly.

// How It Works From rough idea to living discussion

Pick your role

Sign in as a student or a lecturer. Each role comes with the abilities that suit it, from everyday writing to looking after the community.

Write your story

Draft in an editor that happily handles code snippets, pictures, and neat formatting. Publish the moment it feels ready.

Reach your readers

Your article joins a browsable campus library organised by category, with search and a trending shelf pointing people straight to the good stuff.

Keep the conversation alive

Readers comment, reply, and save your piece. You hear about it the moment they do — instantly, not eventually.
