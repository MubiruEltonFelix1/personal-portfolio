Toka Events

Project Description

Toka is a social event discovery platform built for African cities, helping people discover events that match their interests, connect with attendees before arrival, and relive moments after the event.

It improves local event participation by simplifying discovery, registration, and attendance while supporting mobile-money-friendly payments for wider access.

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: local event journeys are often fragmented across spreadsheets, chat apps, and manual coordination. Solution: Toka unifies discovery, registration, and community interaction into one social-first flow.
