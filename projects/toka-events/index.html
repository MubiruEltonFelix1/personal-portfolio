// The Problem Finding something to do shouldn't feel like detective work

African cities buzz with things to see and do every week. But hearing about any of it usually means digging through group chats, forwarded messages, and half-updated spreadsheets — and hoping you didn't miss the real plan buried three days deep.

Even once you've picked an event, the journey stays messy. Sorting who's actually coming happens across endless separate conversations. Signing up and paying can involve manual coordination that quietly talks people out of going at all.

The cost is real: great local events stay under-attended, and the people who would have loved them most simply never hear about them in time.
