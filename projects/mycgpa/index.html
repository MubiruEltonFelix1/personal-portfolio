Overview

MyCGPA is a purpose-built academic planning tool for university students. It solves a real problem: knowing where you stand academically and what it will take to reach your goals. Instead of manual spreadsheet calculations or opaque university portals, MyCGPA gives a clear, visual picture of past performance and future possibilities.

Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework overhead — with Chart.js providing clean, responsive visualisations of your academic trajectory.

Tech Stack

How It Works

Add Courses

Enter your current semester courses with credit units and grades.

See Your Standing

View your current semester GPA and cumulative CGPA at a glance.

Set a Target

Input a target CGPA and discover exactly what grades you need in future semesters.

Visualise

Interactive Chart.js graphs show your academic trajectory over time.

Core Features

CGPA Calculation

Accurate computation of semester GPA and cumulative CGPA based on credit units and grade points.

Grade Forecasting

Model future semesters and see what grades you need to achieve your target CGPA before graduation.

What-If Analysis

Experiment with different grade scenarios in upcoming semesters to plan your study strategy.

Visual Reports

Chart.js-powered graphs that make your academic progress easy to understand and share.
