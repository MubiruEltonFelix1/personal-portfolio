// The Problem Most students find out too late

Ask most students how their CGPA is trending and you'll get a shrug. University portals tend to reveal the number once — after the semester is already finished, when nothing can be changed about it.

So people improvise. A hand-built spreadsheet full of formulas nobody fully trusts. Mental arithmetic that quietly falls apart as credit units pile up. Or worse: simply hoping it all works out.

The result? Semesters drift by without ever knowing whether your goal is still within reach — and by the time you find out, the exams that mattered are behind you.
