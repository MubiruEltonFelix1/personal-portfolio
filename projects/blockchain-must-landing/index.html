// The Problem Blockchain sounds brilliant — until you try to understand it

Digital currency is everywhere in the news: coins soaring, prices crashing, fortunes made overnight. Yet for most people at Mbarara University of Science and Technology, the actual subject feels locked behind a wall of strange words and confident opinions. You want to understand it — you just don't know where a beginner is supposed to start.

The explanations that do exist tend to assume you already know the basics. They jump straight into charts, codes and acronyms, and a curious reader gives up before learning anything useful. The result: interest stays high, but understanding stays low.

What the campus community needed was a welcoming front door — one simple page that says "here is what this actually means," in words you already use every day.

// The Payoff What you get when you land here

Made for the MUST community
