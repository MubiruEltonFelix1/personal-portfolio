// The Problem Quiz Nights Keep Dying Before They Start

Picture the last trivia session you joined: half the room stuck downloading an app, others giving up the moment a sign-up form asked for their email. By the time everyone finally gets in, the energy is gone. Most quiz tools were built for classrooms — designed around a single session, played once, then forgotten.

And when the game ends, everything ends with it. Scores vanish, teams scatter, and next week the organizer starts from zero. There's no simple way to keep a rivalry alive across weeks, or to reward the team that actually showed up every single time.

Worst of all, when scoring lags behind the shouting, the magic dies. A live quiz should feel like a game show — the answer lands, the score flips, the room erupts. If the leaderboard catches up ten seconds later, nobody cares anymore.
