What is BrainBolt?

BrainBolt is a real-time quiz arena where hosts launch live trivia battles in under 60 seconds and players join instantly from any device — no downloads, no account creation, just a 6-character code. Used by 2,400+ teams across 30+ countries with 120,000+ questions answered.

Three modes cover every use case: Solo Sprint for individual training and arcade-style practice, Team Clash for corporate events and remote team engagement, and League Mode for multi-week seasonal tournaments with persistent leaderboards and automated prize distribution.
