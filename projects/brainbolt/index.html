What is BrainBolt?

BrainBolt is a real-time quiz platform that turns any room into a live game show.

You launch a quiz from your device — phone, laptop, tablet — and players join instantly with a 6-character code. No apps to download, no accounts to create. They just open their browser, tap the code in, and start answering. Scores update live, leaderboards shift with every response, and within seconds the room is fully engaged.

It works for everything from a quick icebreaker at a team standup to a multi-week league tournament spanning dozens of teams. Solo Sprint for self-paced individual play, Team Clash for group battles, and League Mode for persistent seasons with automated standings. One platform, every format.
