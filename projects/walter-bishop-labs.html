WALTER-BISHOP-LABS

A research and innovation initiative focused on building probabilistic reasoning systems for high-stakes, real-world decisions. The flagship implementation is SEPSIS-BAYES.

Project Summary

WALTER-BISHOP-LABS is built to make uncertainty actionable through transparent Bayesian reasoning systems. It focuses on explainability and practical deployment, starting with healthcare use cases.

Its flagship project, SEPSIS-BAYES, continuously updates a patient's probability of sepsis using contextual priors (hospital, ward type, age, recent surgery, known infection) plus streaming evidence (vitals and lab observations).

What It Does

  • Computes a prior sepsis probability from patient context at admission.
  • Applies Bayesian updates as new evidence arrives over time.
  • Maintains a dynamic patient state with evidence history.
  • Supports threshold-based clinical escalation (e.g., monitoring, antibiotics, sepsis bundle).
  • Provides explainable, traceable risk updates instead of opaque binary predictions.

Features

  • Dynamic Bayesian updating with sequential evidence integration.
  • Contextual priors informed by hospital and patient risk factors.
  • Minimal, realistic hospital schema (patients table + observations table).
  • Real-time simulation workflow and threshold-driven intervention logic.
  • Roadmap for calibration, dashboard integration, and multi-disease expansion.
