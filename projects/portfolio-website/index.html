// The Problem Great Work Deserves More Than a Plain List of Links

Most people meet a developer through a pile of disconnected things: a resume that fits on one page, a profile full of repository names nobody opens, and maybe a message thread somewhere. The actual work — the part worth talking about — ends up hidden behind links that all look exactly the same.

Recruiters skim. Students and collaborators click away fast. If a visitor can't understand who you are and what you build within seconds, they simply move on — and the best projects never get their moment.

The hard part wasn't adding more decoration. It was combining genuine visual polish with a structure simple enough to keep updated — so the site stays sharp without turning into a chore to maintain.
